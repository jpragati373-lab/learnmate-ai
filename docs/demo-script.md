# Two-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:15 — The problem

“Students can get an explanation and still not know what they misunderstood. Practice is often random, weak topics stay hidden, and revision happens too late. LearnMate AI closes that gap.”

## 0:15–0:30 — The product loop

Open the landing page, enter the dashboard, and show the Computer Networks learning path. Point out the current topic, weak topic, recommendation, and revision due today.

## 0:30–0:50 — Understand

Open Learn, enter **Subnetting**, and generate the explanation. Quickly show the simple explanation, analogy, worked example, common mistakes, and knowledge checks. Mention the visible AI-content verification notice.

## 0:50–1:15 — Practice

Click **Quiz Me**, start the five-question practice session, intentionally choose an incorrect answer, and show the instant explanation. Complete the quiz to display the score and adaptive difficulty path.

## 1:15–1:30 — Improve

On the results screen, show **Weak topic detected**, **Review topic**, **Ask LearnMate AI**, and **View revision**. Highlight the recommendation to practise Subnetting because the demo accuracy is 48%.

## 1:30–1:45 — Remember

Open Revision and show Subnetting due today with its high-priority review. Explain that quiz performance feeds the review schedule instead of using a random reminder.

## 1:45–1:55 — Measure

Open Analytics and show accuracy, topic performance, trend, activity, and the next best action. Emphasize that the metrics come from the demo learning records.

## 1:55–2:00 — Contextual support and close

Open the assistant and ask: **“Explain subnetting in a simpler way.”** Close with: **“LearnMate AI does not simply answer students’ questions. It creates a learning loop that explains difficult concepts, tests understanding, identifies weaknesses, recommends what to study next, schedules revision, and measures improvement.”**
