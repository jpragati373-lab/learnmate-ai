# Final two-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:15 — Problem

“Students can read an explanation and still not know what they misunderstood. Practice is often random, weak concepts stay hidden, and revision happens too late.”

## 0:15–0:30 — Dashboard and learning path

Open the dashboard and then the Computer Networks learning path. Point out Subnetting at 48%, TCP vs UDP at 91%, OSI Model at 64%, the recommendation to practise Subnetting, and the revision due today.

## 0:30–0:50 — AI concept explanation

Open Learn, enter **Subnetting**, and generate the explanation. Show the simple explanation, analogy, example, common mistakes, and knowledge checks. Point out the AI-generated/demo disclosure.

## 0:50–1:15 — Adaptive quiz

Click **Quiz Me**, start the five-question quiz, intentionally select an incorrect answer, and show the immediate explanation. Complete the quiz to show the score and adaptive difficulty feedback.

## 1:15–1:30 — Weakness and recommendation

Show the quiz result: weak-topic detection, the 48% accuracy, **Practice Subnetting**, Review Topic, Ask LearnMate AI, and View Revision actions.

## 1:30–1:45 — Smart revision

Open Revision and show that Subnetting is due for review with high priority. Explain that quiz performance informs the schedule.

## 1:45–1:55 — Analytics

Open Analytics and show topic performance, accuracy, activity, trends, retention indicator, and next best action. Describe these as demo learning metrics, not scientific claims.

## 1:55–2:00 — Assistant and closing

Open the contextual assistant and ask: **“Explain subnetting in a simpler way.”**

Close with: **“LearnMate AI doesn't just explain what students don't know. It identifies what they don't understand, recommends what they should practise next, and helps them retain it.”**
