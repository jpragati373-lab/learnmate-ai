# Two-minute pitch

**0:00–0:20 — Problem:** Students often read an explanation without knowing whether they understood it. Practice can be random, weak concepts remain hidden, and revision is easy to forget.

**0:20–0:40 — Solution:** LearnMate AI connects the full loop: understand, practise, evaluate, detect weakness, get a recommendation, revise, and measure progress. The explanation and practice adapt to the learner instead of treating every student the same.

**0:40–1:20 — Live demonstration:** I choose Computer Networks and open Subnetting. LearnMate gives a clear explanation with an analogy, example, common mistakes, and knowledge checks. I open a five-question practice set, intentionally miss one answer, and immediately see why it was wrong. The results identify Subnetting as weak, recommend practising it, and provide a path to ask the assistant for a simpler explanation.

**1:20–1:40 — What makes it intelligent:** AI helps generate explanations, questions, and contextual coaching. But the product does not let a model decide a student's score or revision date. Deterministic application logic calculates accuracy, weak topics, adaptive difficulty, recommendations, revision scheduling, and analytics. If the provider is unavailable, clearly labelled demo content keeps the journey usable.

**1:40–1:55 — Responsible AI and accessibility:** We disclose AI-generated content, ask students to verify important information, keep provider keys server-side, validate inputs, and minimise personal data. The interface uses labelled controls, keyboard focus states, readable contrast, and text summaries for charts.

**1:55–2:00 — Closing:** LearnMate AI does not simply answer students' questions. It closes the gap between understanding a concept and actually mastering it.
