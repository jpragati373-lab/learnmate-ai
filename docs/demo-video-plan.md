# Final demo video plan

**Target duration:** approximately 2 minutes  
**Status:** Not recorded.

## Opening — 10–15 seconds

Show the landing page. Narrate the student problem: explanations alone do not reveal misunderstanding, and random practice hides weak topics.

## Product overview — 15 seconds

Show the dashboard and learning path. Narrate the continuous loop: understand, practise, evaluate, recommend, revise, and measure.

## Live demo — 70–80 seconds

1. Open Computer Networks and Subnetting.
2. Generate the explanation.
3. Show the analogy, example, and knowledge checks.
4. Click Quiz Me.
5. Submit one deliberately incorrect answer.
6. Show instant explanation and correct-answer feedback.
7. Complete the quiz and show weak-topic detection.
8. Show Practice Subnetting recommendation.
9. Open Revision and show Subnetting due today.
10. Open Analytics and show topic performance and insights.

## AI differentiation — 15 seconds

Explain that AI generates explanations, questions, and contextual tutoring, while deterministic application logic owns scoring, classifications, recommendations, revision timing, and analytics.

## Closing — 10 seconds

Open the assistant and ask for a simpler explanation of Subnetting. Close with: “LearnMate AI doesn't just explain what students don't know. It identifies what they don't understand, recommends what they should practise next, and helps them retain it.”
